Information Sheet

Please read the following information carefully before deciding whether to participate.

Study Title: Promotion Decisions in Management Departments

Protocol Number:AAAR2931(M00Y02)

Purpose of the study

The purpose of this study is to have a better understanding of workplace decisions.

What information is on this form?

We are asking you to take part in a research study. This form explains why we are doing this study and generally what you will be asked to do if you choose to be in this study. This form will not cover all the details about the study prior to your participation, however, you will be debriefed with more information at the end of the study. It also describes the way we (Researchers) would like to use and share information about you. Please take the time to read this form. We will talk to you about taking part in this research study. You should ask us any questions you have about this form and about this research study. You do not have to participate if you don't want to.

Why is this study being done?

We are doing this research study to better understand how people think about certain workplace decisions and decision-making processes.

What will I be asked to do if I choose to be in this study?

We will ask you to complete a survey where you may read vignettes, recall personal experiences, or answer general questions related to certain workplace decisions, and you may be asked how you felt regarding the topics presented. At the end of the study, we will explain in greater detail what we hope to learn from this research. This study will last about 10 minutes.

Use of Data:

We would like to store the data that you agreed to provide as part of this study and possibly use them for future research. They will be stored in an encrypted file on the Investigator's computer. There will be no identifiable information attached to your data.

Are there any risks?

We do not think that there are any risks to taking part in this. Though we will try our best to ensure confidentiality there is a low risk that confidentiality will be compromised.

Are there benefits to taking part in the study?

You will not receive personal (direct) benefit from taking part in this research study. However, the information collected from this research may help others in the future.

What about my privacy?

Every effort will be made to keep your personal information confidential. However, we cannot guarantee total privacy. The data collected will be given a code number, and separated from your name or any other information that could identify you. The research file will be kept in a password protected database. Only the Principal Investigator and the study staff will be able to see this file. If information from this study is published or presented at scientific meetings, your name and other personal information about you will not be used.

Will I get paid or be given anything to take part in this study?

We will give you $2.00 to pay you for your time.

Will I incur costs if I take part in this study?

There will be no costs to you for being in this study

Do I have to be in the study?

Participation in this study is voluntary. Refusal to participate will involve no penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled. You may discontinue participation at any time without penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled.

Who can I call if I have questions?

You may email Isaac Raymundo at iraymundo26@gsb.columbia.edu if you have any questions or concerns about this research study. If you have any questions about your rights as a research participant, or if you have a concern about this study, you may contact the Institutional Review Board at askirb@columbia.edu.
